# Operating Signature — V2 Methodology

## Originality

The assessment items in Operating Signature V2 were written specifically for this project. They are not copied from Generalist World, Elevation Chief of Staff, 16Personalities, MBTI, Enneagram, or another archetype assessment.

Those products were references for the broader category and experience question: how can a person understand patterns in how they operate? Operating Signature uses its own constructs, scenarios, wording, scoring model, result architecture, and visual experience.

## What V2 maps

V2 uses ten independent operating modes:

1. Systems Thinker
2. Builder
3. Analyst
4. Strategist
5. Synthesizer
6. Innovator
7. Operator
8. Translator
9. Connector
10. Explorer

The product architecture is:

**Operating Modes → Operating Signature → Primary Pattern**

A person is not forced into one mutually exclusive type. Multiple modes can be strong at the same time.

## Question design

V2 contains 30 original scenario-based items across six contexts:

- Seeing
- Making
- Deciding
- Communicating
- Working
- Exploring

Each response contributes a stronger signal to one operating mode and a supporting signal to another. The goal is to reduce obvious self-labeling such as “I am a systems thinker” and instead observe patterns through choices made in realistic situations.

## Scoring

Each mode is scored independently against the maximum signal exposure available to that mode in the current question bank. Scores are therefore internal **expression scores from 0–100**, not shares of a pie and not population percentiles.

The highest-scoring modes become the dominant signals. The top combination is then translated into a Primary Pattern such as **System Builder**, **Systems Strategist**, **Meaning Maker**, or **Possibility Scout**.

## Important beta limitation

Operating Signature V2 is an exploratory product prototype, not yet a validated psychometric instrument. Its scores should not be described as diagnostic, clinical, scientifically validated, or population-normed.

Future validation should examine item quality, reliability, construct validity, test-retest stability, measurement invariance/bias, factor structure, and score calibration using real participant data.
