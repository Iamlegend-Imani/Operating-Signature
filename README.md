# Operating Signature

**You are not one type. You are a pattern.**

Operating Signature is a multidimensional assessment that maps how a person naturally tends to see complexity, build, decide, communicate, connect, and explore.

Instead of assigning one fixed archetype, it maps ten operating modes independently and reveals the strongest combination as a **Primary Pattern**.

## The 10 operating modes

1. Systems Thinker
2. Builder
3. Analyst
4. Strategist
5. Synthesizer
6. Innovator
7. Operator
8. Translator
9. Connector
10. Explorer

## The experience

- 33 scenario-based questions
- 10 independent operating-mode scores
- 3 signature signals
- 1 Primary Pattern, such as **System Builder**
- a living radar / signature map
- full composition view
- strengths and growth-edge interpretation
- saveable share card
- saved results on the device without requiring an account
- Atmosphere controls for feel, temperature, brightness, and opacity
- responsive mobile and desktop experience

## Product architecture

**Operating Modes → Operating Signature → Primary Pattern**
